# SaaS Growth Pack

This pack contains the vertical-specific configurations for B2B SaaS clients.

## Included Assets
- **ICPs**: Engineering Managers, VPs of Marketing, Procurement.
- **KPIs**: MQLs, SQLs, Demo Requests, Churn Rate, CAC.
- **Workflows**: LinkedIn Lead Generation, G2 Review Scraping, Cold Email Outreach.
- **Constraints**: GDPR compliance checks, strict tone guidelines for enterprise outreach.
